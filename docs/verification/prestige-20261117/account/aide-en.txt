How do I request an estimate?

Open Request an estimate, describe your project and attach your photos or documents. You can review the summary before sending.

Where can I find my cart?

The Cart button in the header opens the selection saved on this device. You can view it without an account.

Where can I see my orders?

Sign in to My account, then open Orders to view available status updates.

What should I do if I can’t sign in?

Select Forgot password in My account. If the problem continues, select Contact us.

How do I prepare several custom bends?

In Custom metal bending, choose a profile and enter its measurements, finish and quantity. Add your pieces to the cart, then prepare the next bend. Dimensioned drawings stay with your pieces.

Does my cart confirm an order?

Your cart prepares your request. MIR confirms pricing, availability and terms before any order. No online payment is requested at this stage.
